Travel by Month in India


Owing to its geographical diversity, India could be visited throughout the year. Whereas winter months from October till March are the peak season for tourism in India, monsoon is the best time to visit for enjoying the rain in Northern India as well as the hills or plains in the South for Ayurveda therapies. For wildlife adventure, April, May and June months are the best time to visit National Parks in India as it is the time when chances for sighting Bengal tigers are best. Winter months are perfect too for wildlife spotting. Summer months from April till July is best to for hiking, treks and adventure in North and North Eastern Region of India as well as visit to Ladakh. With our compilation on best time to visit in India according to season, know where to plan your holiday and best things to do in that season if you are planning your tour to India.

January in India


January in India marks the beginning of the new year and the peak of winter in India. North Indian regions of the Himalayas are freezing and capital Delhi is pleasantly cold. South Indian destinations like Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Goa are relatively warmer. Some of the best places to visit in January in India are Delhi for the Republic Day parade and Ahmedabad for Kite Festival.With celebrations still on in Goa, the ‘Party Capital of India’ will keep your spirits high. The beaches of Goa will be enchanting enough during this time and will never fail to amaze you even in the winters of January as the tropical climatic condition of Goa.

February in India


The month of Valentine's, February in India is great for honeymooners. The weather is cold in most destinations where couples can spend time in coziness and privacy. February brings great travel times and some of the best places to visit in February in India include Rajasthan, Goa, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, and Kodaikanal.The Himalayas would be mostly out of reach as the roads might still be blocked with snow. The best thing about February though is that it’s a great month to plan a trip to anywhere in India. In case you ever wanted to go for an in-depth trip to India, now it’s the best time to do so!

March in India


March hosts the most colorful festival in India - Holi. The country celebrates this festival with vibrant colors and sweets. March is also the shoulder season in some destinations and great travel deals and discounts can be enjoyed. Mathura, Brindavan, cities in Rajasthan, Kashmir, Leh, Shimla, Alleppey, and Port Blair are some of the best places to visit in March in India.Holi in March is holiday time in India and it is the time when people get to meet their loved ones back at home, play with colors, and relish the best of Indian sweets and savories. The festivities of Holi takes place all over the country.

April in India


Summer knocks at the doors of India in April. A visit to the hill stations in North India like Darjeeling, Nainital, Shimla, and South India like Trivandrum, Coonoor, Munnar is perfect during this time. Most of the popular and offbeat destinations in India can be visited for cooler weather. In April, the famous Char Dhams in Uttarakhand opens up for avid devotees.The beautiful beaches, pristine tropical islands, enchanting hill stations, mysterious jungles and deserts, vibrant cities, interesting heritage sites and a lot more. India, in April, is basically everything you need to take a break from the daily chores of city life .

May in India


From Ladakh in the North to Kerala in the South, a trip to India in May will set your travel goals. This month is perfect to enjoy spectacular landscapes, adventure, and have fun with your friends and family. Destinations like Kasol, Kullu, Kufri, Mussoorie, Wayanad, Alleppey, and Ladakh are a bit cooler and pleasant than the plains.Starting from the topmost part of the country, Ladakh, the unexplored "Land of High Passes" is heaven. The particular remote region is typically the heart of Himalayas clears up its passes within the peak of summertime to receive visitors following long-awaited months.

June in India


Summers get hotter in June and the scorching heat of the plains gets unbearable. This brings perfect times to plan a family holiday to hill stations including Manali, Shimla, Nainital, Coorg, Munnar, and Srinagar. Spend some quality time with your loved ones amidst the tranquil nature.In June, the season for the summer holidays in India reaches its crescendo. The schools and colleges get closed during this time and hence, June becomes a good time to travel with family in India. Since it is also the peak summer month with monsoons, there are a few places where one can travel for a much relaxed and fulfilling summer vacation in India.

July in India


With the onset of July in India, the monsoon picks up its pace. Verdant places like Khandala, Lonavala, Valley of Flowers, Udaipur, Jaipur, and Kodaikanal bloom into youthful beauty. Early monsoon showers enhance the charm of the beautiful environs.The Himalayan mountain ranges will provide you with some gut-wrenching adventures which you must be craving for at this hour! You will be able to actually experience the beauty of rains outside, without having to sit at home, or inside the office and seeing the beauty from inside through the windows. To satiate the adrenaline rush, one can head straight to Leh Ladakh .

August in India


The unique spectacles and precious gems of India bloom with lush greenery upon the arrival of the monsoon in August in India. With the downpour in August, the charm of the Ooty, Khajuraho, Pachmarhi, Darjeeling, Pahalgam, and Kanyakumari reaches its peak.The best month for getting the benefits of Ayurvedic treatments in Kerala is surely August. Apart from rejuvenating the senses, planning a trip to ‘God’s Own Country’ in this month treats you to the best sights and sounds. Exploring Varkala, Kannur, Wayanad or Munnar is an amazing option. Sailing across the backwaters in Kumarakom on traditional houseboats.

September in India


September in India hosts important religious festivals including Ganesh Chaturthi. During this month, the temperature in India remains pleasant clear blue skies, which is perfect for sightseeing and activities. September is the last month of monsoon in India and places from North to South experience a tropical climate. You can plan your visit to Kerala, Ladakh, Mumbai, and Rajasthan in September.September is also the month in which you can think of traversing the Golden Triangle in India. Taking you to Delhi, Jaipur in Rajasthan and Agra in Uttar Pradesh, the most-favored circuit offers matchless experiences.

October in India


The month of October in India is welcoming - both culturally and climatically. It is the time when the monsoon in India ends and the winter season begins. Festivals and cultural events take place in Rajasthan and Arunachal Pradesh. One can indulge in auspicious Dussehra, Navratri, and Durga Puja festivals. October marks the starting of the best time for visiting India's all-region from east to west and north to south.While the atmosphere plays a very important role while choosing the best time to travel in India in October, another thing which you must remember is that it is the month of festivals.

November in India


November acts as a tourist magnet for foreign tourists since it is the second-best month to visit India. The wind starts getting cooler and the skies become clearer. November invites surfers to pristine beaches in India and snow lovers can head to Himachal Pradesh, Sikkim, Ladakh, and Uttarakhand. Find migratory birds and go for wild safari trips. Be a part of major festival celebrations in November including Dussehra, Diwali, and Chhath Puja.It is also the month of holidays in India and hence, a lot of leaves from the workplace. So, in case you have been waiting to go on a vacation, this is probably the perfect time for you to do so.

December in India


While the North Indian regions like Ladakh, Himachal, and Sikkim are enveloped in snow, the beginning of December provides a long hour of sunshine in South India. For adventure enthusiasts, trekking is best done in December. Plus, many honeymooners visit Manali, Shimla, Munnar, Goa in India to romance amidst the pleasant and cool weather.A visit to Kohima, in Nagaland, for the famous Hornbill Festival can stay in your memories for a great period of time. However, you need to book your tickets early for this as people from all over the world visits this festival during this time.
